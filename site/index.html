Product Design for companies that need to scale, adapt and explore.

Amsterdam-based product design specialist creating well-crafted digital products on visual systems that scale. Over the last 15 years, I've led and worked in multidisciplinary teams, shipping work for industry-leading companies and for startups searching for product-market fit. My main goal is to create work that's simple, yet impactful, this usually means systems need to be easily understood and the visual execution stands the test of time. I work best as a senior IC or in a hands-on leadership role, where I can foster an environment for collaboration and exploration.
