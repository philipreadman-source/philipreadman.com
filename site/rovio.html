Designing a joy-first approach to a player-focused gaming company.

Big Picture

Rovio needed to refresh their website and employer branding to reflect their personality and culture, and showcase the innovation and craft behind their games.

Role

As Design Lead I worked hands-on designing the user experience and visual design from the ground up. Collaborating with the Rovio recruitment team I delivered a redesign that worked seamlessly with their CRM and showcased the innovation and cultural integrity of the brand.

Approach

Position Rovio around their core values: playful, credible and creative. Bold, impactful layouts and prominent use of character art enabled Rovio to appear both serious and playful to potential recruits and partners.

Outcome

A Webby Award-nominated website that enabled internal teams to create beautiful pages, faster.

● Continued growth of key hires
● Successful acquisition by SEGA (Aug 2023)
